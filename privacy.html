Allfolio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Allfolio (previously Astrolabe Portfolio) is designed as a private portfolio and bookkeeping app. We do not operate a backend account system for the app, and we do not sell user data.

Information You Store In The App

You may enter portfolio holdings, asset names, transaction records, bookkeeping records, recurring plans, notes, currencies, and related financial details. This data is stored on your device. If iCloud Auto Sync is enabled, it is also stored in your own private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit service.

We do not run our own server to collect or host these core records.

iCloud Sync

iCloud sync is provided by Apple. Your synced app data is associated with your Apple Account and stored in your private iCloud database. The availability of iCloud sync depends on your iCloud account status, device settings, network connection, and Apple's services.

AI Recognition And Analysis

If you choose to use AI recognition for transaction or trade parsing, the text you provide for recognition may be sent to the configured AI service, currently DeepSeek, so the service can return structured results. If you choose to use AI portfolio analysis, a summary of your portfolio holdings and related financial details may be sent to DeepSeek so the service can generate an analysis response. Do not submit sensitive information that you do not want processed by that third-party provider.

Market Data And News

When you search symbols, refresh prices, exchange rates, historical prices, or news, the app may send the relevant symbol, search query, or asset name to external market data or news providers such as Yahoo Finance, Tencent Finance, Bing News, or similar services. These providers process requests under their own privacy terms.

Subscriptions And Purchases

Subscriptions, free trials, payments, refunds, and cancellation flows are handled by Apple through the App Store. The app uses Apple's StoreKit framework to check entitlement status. We do not receive your full payment card information.

Diagnostics And Analytics

Allfolio includes first-party usage analytics to improve onboarding, subscriptions and reliability. Sharing is enabled by default and can be turned off on the subscription screen or in Settings → Usage Analytics. It is independent of portfolio iCloud Auto Sync and does not require a Pro subscription.

Events include app opens, onboarding completion, subscription-screen views, subscription product loading and timing, purchase success, cancellation, pending approval or numeric error codes, verified trial or paid access, and whether the first asset entry or cash-linked transaction was completed. Records include the app version, build, App Store region, random installation and session identifiers, whether this is a new or earlier installation, and trial eligibility or expiry when available. We do not include asset names, holdings, amounts, transaction contents, screenshots, contact details, full error descriptions, payment-card information or StoreKit transaction identifiers in these events.

Events are sent through Apple's CloudKit to the app's developer-managed analytics records when iCloud is available. Access is restricted to the developer and the creating account; other app users cannot read your events. CloudKit attaches an account-scoped creator identifier, so these records may be linked to an account within this app's container. We do not use this information for advertising, cross-app tracking, profiling across services or data sales, and we do not request an advertising identifier.

Pending events are stored separately from your portfolio, limited to 512 events and 30 days. Turning sharing off stops future analytics collection and upload, clears unsent events, and rotates the installation identifier before sharing can resume. It does not retrospectively erase events already received. Uploaded records are used for product analysis and troubleshooting. Analytics failure does not block app access, purchases, or saving your records. Apple may separately provide aggregated App Store and crash information according to your Apple settings and Apple's policies.

Feedback

If you submit feedback, the message, optional email address or WeChat contact and displayed diagnostic context (app and system version, device model, language, App Store region, subscription status, sync setting and display currency) are sent to the app's developer through CloudKit for customer support. CloudKit attaches an account-scoped creator identifier, so feedback may be linked to the creating account within this app's container. This separate, user-initiated feature does not attach portfolio or transaction records.

Your Choices

  • You can disable iCloud Auto Sync in the app settings.
  • You can turn off Share Usage Analytics on the subscription screen or in Settings → Usage Analytics.
  • You can delete app data from your device by deleting records in the app or removing the app from your device.
  • You can manage subscriptions in iOS Settings under your Apple Account subscriptions.

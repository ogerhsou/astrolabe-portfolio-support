Data And iCloud Sync

  • Your core portfolio, transaction, ledger, and recurring plan data is stored on your device.
  • When iCloud Auto Sync is enabled and you are signed in to iCloud, the app syncs through your private iCloud database.
  • If sync does not appear on another device, confirm that both devices use the same iCloud account and have iCloud enabled in iOS Settings.

Subscriptions

  • Subscriptions and free trials are managed by Apple through the App Store.
  • You can manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings under your Apple Account subscriptions.
  • If a purchase does not unlock the app immediately, restart the app and make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy for details about local storage, iCloud sync, AI recognition, market data requests, and App Store subscription handling.

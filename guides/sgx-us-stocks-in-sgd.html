Track SGX and US stocks in SGD

Your US broker reports dollars and your Singapore broker reports Singapore dollars. Keep the original balances so you can check each statement, then choose SGD for the combined view.

Give each currency its own cash account

In Allfolio, add your Singapore holding under Singapore Stocks and your US holding under US Stocks. Give each broker’s cash account a recognisable name, such as “Singapore cash · SGD” and “US cash · USD”. Choose SGD as the display currency in Settings.

Record a US$1,000 purchase

Suppose the Singapore account holds 100 shares at S$20 and S$1,000 cash. The US account starts with US$2,000 cash. Record a buy of 10 US shares at US$100, turn on the cash update and select the USD cash account.

Check the combined value

At an illustrative rate of S$1.30 per US$1, the US account is worth S$2,600. Add the S$3,000 Singapore account and the two accounts total S$5,600. The purchase moves money from cash to shares. This example holds both prices and the exchange rate fixed and excludes fees.

Check each broker’s closing cash in the currency printed on the statement. You can run the free cash calculator once for SGD and once for USD.
